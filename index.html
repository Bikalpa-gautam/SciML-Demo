Concrete defect recognition: live demo

Upload a photo of a concrete surface. Two networks look at it side by side: our own CNN (1.6 M parameters) and a fine-tuned ResNet-18 (11.2 M). Everything runs in this browser; the photo is not uploaded anywhere.

How to read this, and the limits
  • Real bridge photos: each label (crack, rust, spalling) gets its own probability; a photo can have several. A label counts as detected when its probability passes the threshold tuned on the validation photos (the tick on each bar).
  • Synthetic task: one of four classes; the probabilities add up to 100 %. These models never saw a real photo, so on real photos they are unreliable (mAP ≈ 0.50, guessing ≈ 0.42): this is the domain gap.
  • Image-level labels only: the networks say whether a defect is in the photo, not where, and not the crack width or type.
  • 224 × 224 pixels: thin cracks are the hardest class (crack AP 0.62 for the ResNet, 0.55 for the own CNN on 975 test photos).
  • A student research demo, not a tool for structural assessment.
